Another Czech translation of Iain Banks! And this one... was not bad? But I would say that it was not my favourite. The concept of two stories set in the same world is interesting, but when I finished, it was more of a "what was that?" than a "WOW". I would rate this as the least interesting in the series (so far). Still, I am glad that Planeta9 released the translation.
